MSFS source, formalization and library audit — 2026-10-22

This is a defect register, not a change to the paper or a relaxation of its
statements. Existing foreign scaffolds remain migration evidence. They do not
become proofs when their labels, citation years or framework tags are checked.

O1. Membership and AFNT-alpha are different obligations.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex, lem:SS-membership; the (F_S) definition; thm:afnt-alpha.
The new proof introduces the syntactic branch for the represented pointing
(Syn(S), [phi]). F-3 instead supplies a categorical equivalence involving Syn(F).
An inclusion F in S does not by itself make the interpretation Syn(F)->Syn(S)
fully faithful or establish literal equality of the represented pointings.
Required: a precise ambient category of pointed objects, the comparison map,
and a proof that the ORIGINAL F-3 hypotheses justify the relevant transport.
Adding the desired membership as a hypothesis would not discharge this task.
Status: representation-inclusion checked; full alpha correspondence open.

O2. A definable colimit need not encode a definable presentation tower.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex, proper-class argument following AFNT-beta.
Invalid inference: a formal specification of a colimit determines a formal
specification of every input in its diagram. Counterexample at the recursive
presentation level: choose a noncomputable binary sequence b_n, let A_n be a
pointed finite set with 1+b_n elements, and let every strict transition A_i->A_j
be constant at the distinguished point (identities at i=j). These maps compose.
Every element becomes the distinguished point at the next stage, so the colimit
is the one-point set, with a constant finite specification. Its diagram still
encodes the noncomputable sequence. Thus the inference already fails for a
countable directed diagram. This diagnoses the inference, not the negation of
the paper's full proper-class conclusion.
Required: an actual recovery/faithfulness theorem for the particular tower,
or a different proof of the unchanged conclusion.
Status: open; no certificate.

O3. Nonexplosion quantifier.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex, Explosion-min.
The condition bottom does not derive phi for EVERY phi contradicts reflexivity
at phi=bottom. Nonexplosion means NOT(every phi is derivable from bottom), or
that at least one phi is not derivable. They are different quantifier orders.
Required: explicit author-visible correction of the condition and rechecking
all dependent claims. No current theorem is marked proved by replacing its goal.
Status: defective source condition recorded; paper unchanged in this change.

O4. Unconnected arbitrary outputs in legacy categorical library contracts.
Source: Verum core/math/s_definable/class_s_s.vr and lemma_3_4.vr.
Local membership entails closure_depth=0. The Grothendieck closure axiom takes
arbitrary diagram and result references, requires only nonnegative diagram
depth, and entails result depth>=1. Choosing result=diagram equal to a local
witness gives depth=0 and depth>=1. No relation tying result to a construction
appears in the contract. Similar defects affect other closure markers.
Required: a typed construction with explicit inputs/output relation and an
actual proof of preservation. A citation or integer metadata is insufficient.
The portable Pi/Id source profile does not import these contracts.
Status: source signature inconsistent; cannot serve as certified foundation.

O5. Legacy library kernel is not an implementation of the portable kernel.
Source: Verum core/verify/kernel_v0.
lookup_shifted returns the raw context type; for [A:U0,x:A], lookup of x needs
the shifted type Var1, not Var0. The substitution lemma file contains constant
false typing predicates and a substitution returning the unchanged term. The
multi-step beta predicate is false even at a reflexive step. The native Rust
checker has independent implementations; these library routines are NOT their
verified extraction or proof of soundness.
Required: shared calculus contract, executable capture-avoiding substitution,
shifted lookup, reflexive-transitive reduction, and checked metatheorems. Until
then they cannot certify Verum's implementation.
Status: concrete unimplemented/incorrect stdlib mechanisms; not used by the
source/native Noesis admission path.

O6. Reflection/stabilization authority.
Source: Verum predicative_reflection.vr, reflection_tower.rs and legacy audits.
Numerical strata, annotation strings and bibliographic framework presence do
not prove stabilization, foundational equivalence or self-soundness. Some
source axioms still refer to claims withdrawn or qualified in the current paper.
Required: statement-bound proof replay, explicit assumption closure and checked
interpretations between foundations. Structural reports must retain their data
but cannot grant discharge authority. See the accompanying Verum authority fix.
Status: proof obligations open; numerical/citation evidence is structural only.

The registry is a focused audit, not a claim that every MSFS or stdlib defect has
been enumerated. Full categorical closure, dual enactments, accessibility bounds,
foreign proof translation and the legacy marker chain remain unverified.
