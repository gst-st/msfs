O1. Membership and AFNT-alpha subtheory transport — RESOLVED IN SOURCE PAPER.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex & paper-ru/paper.tex, lem:SS-membership; def:pi3max; thm:afnt-alpha.
Resolution: Lemma lem:SS-membership now explicitly distinguishes direct / slice-conservative
presentations from arbitrary proper subtheories iota_F : F -> S (noting non-conservativity
of PA -> PA + Con(PA) on slices). Definition def:pi3max states the generative typing
explicitly, and Theorem thm:afnt-alpha proves BOTH (i) the direct / slice-conservative
mechanism (alpha_0) refuting (F_S) & (Pi_4) and (ii) Strong Diagonal Anti-Absoluteness
(alpha_diag) refuting (F_S) & (Pi_{3-max}) for arbitrary faithful subtheories iota_F : F -> S
via T_S := PA + Con(S) in F (witnessed by standard N in U_1 subset U_2 via (R3), without
omega-consistency), single-sorted arithmetic pullback S_arith >= Q + Con(S), Solovay
I*Delta_0 + Omega_1 cut, and Feferman–Pudlak Second Incompleteness (Feferman 1960; Pudlak 1985).
Status: resolved in paper-en/paper.tex and paper-ru/paper.tex (and mechanically formalized in
sibling math-foundations/lean4-mf-kernel/MathFoundations/Metatheory/StrongAFNT.lean); Verum
portable proofss/ss-membership covers the representation-level introduction branch.

O2. Proper-class and non-uniform tower dichotomy — RESOLVED IN SOURCE PAPER.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex & paper-ru/paper.tex, prop:proper-class + terminal-collapse counterexample.
Resolution: Replaced the invalid diagram-recovery inference with the unconditional colimit
R-S-definability dichotomy directly on the limit object A_infty (either exists S' in R-S with
(F_{S'})(A_infty), refuted by thm:afnt-alpha at S', or forall S' in R-S not (F_{S'})(A_infty),
failing the first conjunct of def:level6), accompanied by the explicit terminal-collapse
counterexample A_k (cardinality 1 + b_k collapsing to {*}) showing why diagram uniformity
cannot be recovered from colimit definability.
Status: resolved in paper-en/paper.tex and paper-ru/paper.tex.

O3. Nonexplosion quantifier — RESOLVED IN SOURCE PAPER AND CORPUS DOCS.
Source: paper-en/paper.tex & paper-ru/paper.tex, def:paraconsistent-rs (Explosion-min);
verum-corpus/src/theorems/msfs/paraconsistent.vr.
Resolution: Corrected Explosion-min from the self-contradictory universal quantifier to

in thm:paraconsistent-afnt.
Status: resolved in paper-en/paper.tex, paper-ru/paper.tex, and paraconsistent.vr.
